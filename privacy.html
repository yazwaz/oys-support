OYS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy describes how the OYS app (“OYS”) handles information on your device. OYS is designed to keep your inventory information local.

Local storage

Inventory information and associated media are stored locally on your device. OYS does not require an internet connection to manage your inventory data on the device.

No account required

No account is required to use OYS. You do not need to create a user profile or sign in to add, view, or manage your items.

Advertising and tracking

OYS does not contain advertising or cross-app tracking.

Camera and photos

OYS may use the camera so you can take photos of items, receipts, and storage locations. When you choose an existing image, Apple’s system photo picker may be used to select photos already on your device.

Photos you add are associated with your inventory information and are stored locally with the app’s data on your device.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual items from within the app. You can also use Delete All Data to remove the app’s locally stored inventory information and associated media from the device.

Deleting the OYS app removes its locally stored app data. Files you previously exported elsewhere (for example, to Files, Photos, email, or another app) are not removed by deleting OYS.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact: oys.support@gmail.com
